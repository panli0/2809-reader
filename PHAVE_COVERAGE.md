# PHaVE 150 coverage audit

Source list: [PHaVE List — Phrasal Verbs](https://www.norbertschmitt.co.uk/), based on Garnier and Schmitt's PHaVE research materials. This audit checks the 150 target items against the nine chapter texts.

## Result

- PHaVE target items: **150**
- Items found in story text: **150 / 150 (100%)**
- Collocation/context records across `collocations-1.js` through `collocations-10.js`: **284**
- Unique phrase patterns in the context bank: **276**
- Context records that could not be matched to a current story sentence: **0**

The matching check accounts for common inflected verb forms and separable-object constructions (for example, a verb followed by an object and then its particle). It checks the story text, not merely the phrase-bank labels.
